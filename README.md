# PiVerse Hub 🌐🚀
**Lightweight Web3 Metaverse, AI Marketplace & Revenue-Sharing Ecosystem**
*Dioptimalkan untuk Jaringan Pi Network (Pi Browser)*

---

## 📌 Deskripsi Proyek / Project Overview

### 🇮🇩 Bahasa Indonesia
**PiVerse Hub** adalah platform super-app Web3 berbasis ekosistem Pi Network yang menggabungkan konsep *Virtual Real Estate*, *Decentralized AI Marketplace*, *Learn-to-Earn*, dan *Metaverse Social Quest*. 

Platform ini dirancang khusus agar ringan (*lightweight*) dan dapat diakses dengan mudah melalui *smartphone* / Pi Browser, memungkinkan UMKM, kreator digital, dan komunitas untuk bersosialisasi, berdagang, serta mendapatkan imbalan pasif (*passive income*).

**Fitur Utama:**
1. **Virtual Real Estate & Asset Leasing:** Sewa lahan virtual 3D, toko UMKM, dan properti digital.
2. **AI & Creative Marketplace:** Jual-beli aset 3D, prompt AI, avatar, dan musik bebas hak cipta dengan royalti otomatis.
3. **Social Quest & Learn-to-Earn:** Dapatkan imbalan poin/reward dari menyelesaikan kursus, tur virtual, dan event komunitas.
4. **Data Vault (Privacy-First):** Berbagi data anonim secara sukarela untuk imbalan harian.
5. **Guild DAO & Revenue Sharing:** Skema bagi hasil yang adil dan transparan antara pengguna, pengembang, dan komunitas.

---

### 🇬🇧 English
**PiVerse Hub** is a Web3 super-app platform integrated with the Pi Network ecosystem, merging *Virtual Real Estate*, *Decentralized AI Marketplace*, *Learn-to-Earn*, and *Metaverse Social Quests*.

Designed to be ultra-lightweight and mobile-friendly via Pi Browser, PiVerse Hub enables MSMEs (UMKM), creators, and global users to trade, socialize, and earn passive rewards seamlessly.

**Key Features:**
1. **Virtual Real Estate & Asset Leasing:** Rent/lease 3D virtual land, storefronts, and digital assets.
2. **AI & Creative Marketplace:** Trade 3D assets, AI prompts, avatars, and royalty-free music with automated royalties.
3. **Social Quests & Learn-to-Earn:** Earn reward points by completing courses, virtual tours, and community events.
4. **Privacy-First Data Vault:** Voluntarily share anonymous data for daily reward distribution.
5. **Guild DAO & Revenue Sharing:** Fair and transparent revenue sharing model supporting users, developers, and the community.

## 🏗️ Tech Stack

- **Frontend:** Next.js / React (Lightweight Mobile-First Web)
- **Database:** Neon Serverless PostgreSQL
- **Web3 Integration:** Pi Network JS SDK & Thirdweb
- **Hosting & Deployment:** GitHub + Vercel (Free Tier)
- **Environment:** Cloud IDE (Replit / Mobile Browser)

---

## 📈 Model Bagi Hasil / Revenue Sharing Structure

Sistem ekosistem dirancang untuk memberikan keberlanjutan dan keadilan bagi semua pihak:
- **Pengguna / Participant:** Imbalan dari sewa, penjualan karya, quest, dan staking.
- **Komunitas / Organisasi:** Pemeliharaan platform, kas DAO, dan pengembangan fitur.
- **Pengembang / Developer:** Pemeliharaan infrastruktur teknis dan keamanan.
